# Leaver Workflow

## Standard Separation

1. Receive an authorized termination event and effective time.
2. Block sign-in and revoke sessions.
3. Remove privileged roles and high-risk access first.
4. Disable or deprovision connected applications.
5. Remove groups, licenses, delegated access, and authentication methods as approved.
6. Trigger device return, lock, retire, or wipe actions based on ownership.
7. Preserve and transfer business data under approved retention requirements.
8. Notify the manager and application owners.
9. Reconcile all targets and record evidence.

## Involuntary or High-Risk Separation

Use a confidential, tightly timed workflow with HR, legal, security, and management coordination. Do not send premature user notifications.
