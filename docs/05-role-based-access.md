# Role-Based Access

## Model

- **Birthright access:** Minimum access all workers in a defined population receive.
- **Role access:** Access associated with approved job functions.
- **Requestable access:** Additional access requiring owner approval.
- **Privileged access:** Elevated access with stronger controls and review.
- **Exceptional access:** Time-limited deviation with risk approval.

## Role Record

Document role owner, business purpose, eligibility attributes, included entitlements, prohibited combinations, approver, review frequency, and removal trigger.
